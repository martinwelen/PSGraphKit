# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers
pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed
necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
